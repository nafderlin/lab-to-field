    Why “not sure” matters

    Lab ≠ field. Models trained on lab photos (plain background, perfect light) score ~99% in the lab and as low as 9% on real farm photos, while still sounding confident.
    Trained for the field. This MobileNetV3 saw real field photos and backgrounds swapped during training: 64% accuracy on unseen field photos, against 30% for the same model trained on lab photos only.
    Honest confidence. Its confidence is calibrated. Below the line it says “not sure”; on the rest it is right 78% of the time.
